            Set up YouTube uploads

            Google asks every app that uploads to have its own free "client". You make it once, in your own Google account. It takes about 10 minutes.

            1. Make a Google Cloud project. Call it Pucked Video.
            2. Turn on the YouTube Data API v3 and press Enable.
            3. Set up Google Auth: press Get started, App name Pucked Video, your email, Audience External. Then under Audience, press Publish app (otherwise Google signs you out every 7 days). It's fine that Google hasn't verified it.
            4. Make a client: Create client, Application type Desktop app, then Download JSON.
            5. Give that file to Pucked Video and connect your channel:

              Or paste the Client ID and secret

              Google will warn that it hasn't verified the app. It's your own app: press Advanced, then Go to Pucked Video, and allow it to upload videos.

            6. Unlisted links: Google keeps videos uploaded by a new project private until the project passes its free review. Ask Google to review it (pick "I want to request an audit"); it can take a few weeks. Until then, uploads are private, and Upload it myself in YouTube Studio lets you pick Unlisted by hand.
